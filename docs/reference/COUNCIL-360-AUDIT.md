## §0.5 · Doctrinal clarification: the THREE legs of regeneration (2026-07-04, prompted by the
## engineer's discomfort — «la ingeniería real refactoriza de a poco, no regenera toneladas»)
«regeneración» is three legs of DISTINCT value; the doctrine must stop selling them together:
- **(a) Re-derivation as VERIFICATION** — PROVEN THESIS, daily value: `ci --rederive == pin` on a
  clean clone IS the trust mechanism («nadie leyó el código y aun así corresponde al spec»).
  It pays for the apparatus even if it is never regenerated by evolution.
- **(b) N products inherit factory improvements** — PROVEN THESIS, exercised dozens of times
  (Vercel re-skin: 1 codegen change → 3 products re-derived; a new check on a shared station
  → re-pin of all of them). This is the regeneration that ACTUALLY happens and pays off.
- **(c) Regenerating as MAINTENANCE of a product in production** — **OPEN HYPOTHESIS, not a
  thesis**: only `V1__init.sql` exists; live data has never survived a regeneration. Do not
  assert it until `SPEC-EVOLUTION` (§3.2) exists and measures it. Classic incremental refactoring DOES
  exist here, one level up: the CODEGEN is refactored (e.g. MONOLITH-REFACTOR, byte-identical) and
  the SPEC; the generated code is a build artifact (compiler model — nobody refactors `.class`).
  SOTA anchor: the industry migrates artifacts from pets to cattle (containers/IaC/lockfiles); betting
  that app code follows suit is defensible WITH a falsifier, not dogma. Conceded regime: a factory for
  a SINGLE app that never improves → the apparatus is overkill; vibe-coding wins.

## §2.5 · Why the factory doesn't run on its own (clarification, 2026-07-04)
**Meta = the machine-tool; a factory = the DIE mounted on it; the product = the part.**
Nothing is «siempre activo» (there is no daemon): every session stands on meta's workbench because
that's where the ENGINE (`src/metafactory/` + CLI — the thing that runs stations/gates/re-derivation/RTM)
and the column live. The factory is data+codegen: it doesn't run, it IS run. ONE machine and not 5
copies because: (1) one engine fix improves all 5 factories instantly (leg b, measured); (2) the
trust-chain is audited ONCE with eyes — 5 copies = 5 audit surfaces of the most expensive point; (3) it
is the standard model (Gradle/Bazel/Rails-engine) — the only non-standard part is «mismo repo» instead
of «versión declarada», chosen to avoid releases/a version matrix and to verify the WHOLE thing in one
clone. Export = `meta export` (bundle with the engine vendored+pinned, gate: the bundle re-derives ==
pin) — not built, YAGNI until a real colleague exists.
