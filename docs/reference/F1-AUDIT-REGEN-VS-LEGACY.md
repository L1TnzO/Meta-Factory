## 0. The question and the category trap

The engineer asked: **is the factory regenerated by the Meta-Factory better than the legacy one, overall?**
Answering with a single scalar would be dishonest, because the two things are not comparable along one dimension:

- The **legacy** is a **self-contained, mature** factory: it brings its OWN engine (`src/agentfactory/`,
  5,516 LOC), its own Copier scaffolding (53 files), live Docker infra, MCP, 445 tests, 23 ADRs.
- The **regenerated** one is a **thin, spec-driven** factory that rides on the Meta-Factory's substrate
  (`src/metafactory/`, 4,504 LOC). The only substantial thing that is its OWN is a deterministic scaffolder
  (908 LOC) + a `factory.spec.yml` (365 lines) + the station line as data.

Comparing "better/worse, flat out" would mix pears (substrate governance) with apples (product-line
breadth). That's why the verdict is broken down into **three axes** (§4–§6) before collapsing into
a decision (§7).

## 1. Verdict in one line

> **As a deliverable factory-product TODAY, the legacy is better overall**: it is more complete, more
> proven in practice, and runs on its own. **As evidence for the Meta-Factory program, F1 is a big
> success**: the regenerated one is **better GOVERNED and better TESTED per unit** (two verifiers from
> birth, re-derivable proof-sha, venv scrub, domain-agnostic substrate) and demonstrated that the
> substrate generates a **real, typed, gated LangGraph product**. The gap that remains against the
> legacy is **no longer one of foundation but of CONTENT** (live infra, MCP, multi-blueprint, station
> breadth). That is incremental, safe backlog, not a redesign.

## 2. Method (what was verified by blocking, not by prose)

Two independent forensic subagents (one per factory, read-only) + the orchestrator's own verification.
Findings cross-checked and confirmed by hand:
- **Legacy**: its own suite `445 passed / 1 skipped` (run in its `.venv`); the verdict is computed SOLELY
  by the verifier from exit codes (`verify/verifier.py:106`); 3 locks + 4 layers of protection.
- **Regenerated**: live RTM `meta rtm --factory agent-systems-factory` → **COMPLETE 6/6**, every artifact
  `✓` on disk; Lock-2 (`seed/verifier.py recompute`) **ok across the 6 proofs**; 3 hooks present
  (`proof_protect`, `commit_guard`, `stop_proof`), not 1; git clean before and after (zero mutation).

## 7. The orchestrator's verdict (the criterion, without collapsing the nuance)

**If "overall" = deliverable factory today → the LEGACY is better.** It is more complete, self-contained,
with real infra, and seasoned by a product in production. The regenerated one still doesn't run on its
own and its line is thin.

**If "overall" = which factory is better BUILT to last and to scale to N factories → the
REGENERATED one wins on foundation.** Governance, re-derivable trust, and substrate generality are
superior; that is where the Meta-Factory's value lies.

**Decision, without dodging it:** *for the purpose of this audit —knowing the real state of the
Meta-Factory— the F1 result is squarely positive, but the legacy remains the more capable factory.*
The regenerated one did NOT surpass the legacy in capability (nor was it going to, in one increment), but
**it proved the substrate is real**: it emits a genuine, typed, gated LangGraph product, with re-derivable
proofs. The key delta is of a different nature than what "better/worse product" measures:

> **F1 turned the gap against the legacy from a FOUNDATION gap into a CONTENT gap.** Before, the
> doubt was "can the Meta-Factory even generate a real product and test it?" — F1 answers yes.
> What is missing (live infra, MCP, multi-blueprint, s0/s2/s5, 4 products) is **filling in the spec with
> what the legacy already knows**, not re-architecting. That is what makes this result valuable: the
> hard part (trustworthy, domain-agnostic substrate, re-derivable, that emits a gated product) works.

## 8. Strengths / weaknesses, concretely

### Regenerated (Meta-Factory)
**Strengths:** (1) real, mypy-strict, gated product, not a proxy — closes the headline codegen gap.
(2) Re-derivable proof chain with teeth (`compute_proof_sha` excludes venv; `clean_runner` self-tests
against a manipulated pin). (3) Real DES-8 isolation. (4) Re-derived RTM, live COMPLETE 6/6.
(5) Substrate/factory separation that enforces ADR-012 (the LangGraph generator lives in the FACTORY, the
substrate names no tech).
**Weaknesses:** (1) **does not run on its own** — depends on the substrate (4,504 LOC); its own part is
~1 scaffolder. (2) "Generation" = parametric substitution of hardcoded templates, **not Copier nor an
LLM**; a single product shape, `domain/` is emitted empty. (3) Thin line: 6/9 stations, **no live infra or
MCP**, 1 demo product. (4) Leaner harness (3 agents/1 command vs 5/7). (5) Capabilities tested only
against fake-LLM/MemorySaver/InMemory; the production paths (real provider, AsyncPostgresSaver,
OTLP, RAGAS) exist in code but **never run under a gate**.

### Legacy (self-made)
**Strengths:** (1) genuine verify-by-blocking (verifier owns the verdict + 3 locks + 4 layers).
(2) Topology safety over the **compiled** graph (`get_graph()`, BFS, ADR-015 invariant). (3)
RTM derived from artifacts with exit code + anti-facade perimeter. (4) Two tiers with real Docker infra
(ephemeral Neo4j/Postgres). (5) Disciplined honesty (HONEST-STATE separates guaranteed-by-construction
vs. by-measurement vs. never) + a clean generated/domain boundary.
**Weaknesses:** (1) narrow envelope — **tested on only 2 shapes** of spec; opinionated toward ONE product
class. (2) S8 evaluability **permanently PROVISIONAL** (stub scorer `0.0`). (3) ledger/predecessor
**not re-derived** — the teeth depend on the hook chain (with `FACTORY_DEV=1` that tier goes away). (4)
fragile/stale environment (py3.14 venv with an editable install pointing to the old path; tests only pass
with `PYTHONPATH=src`). (5) the GENERATE step for new topologies **is not automated** — it stays a
subagent in the harness by design; W4 (opus reviewer) is a narrated rule with no deterministic shadow.

## 9. What this says about the REAL state of the Meta-Factory

1. **The substrate is real, not vaporware.** Generating the factory proved it in practice: M0–M8 green by
   blocking, a product that compiles and passes mypy-strict in its own venv, `ci --rederive` 6/6.
2. **The trust is well-founded.** Two verifiers from birth + re-derivable proof-sha + venv scrub outdo,
   per unit, the legacy's retrofitted teeth.
3. **Product codegen remains the bottleneck** — but now measurable and bounded: it works for 1 parametric
   blueprint; multi-shape, live infra, and MCP are missing. It is not a design hole; it is spec + templates
   yet to be written.
4. **The substrate's generality (ADR-012) held up under real pressure**: the substrate did not end up
   naming LangGraph; the tech stayed in the plan. That was the central architectural bet, and it held.
